How the table works

Rules

Field play

  • Each possession has four downs. Flick from where the ball lies.
  • A touchdown is 6 when the ball comes to rest with any part hanging over the opponent’s end — and the center of mass still on the table.
  • If the ball falls off any edge, it is a turnover. The opponent starts on their own edge.
  • Turnover on downs: the opponent keeps the ball where it stopped and flicks the other way.

Kicking

  • After a touchdown, one extra-point kick from midfield is worth 1.
  • On fourth down you may attempt a field goal worth 3 from the current spot, or go for the touchdown.
  • Stand the triangle on a point and flick it through the finger posts. Over the thumbs and between the uprights is good — including a kick that sails a bit above the fingertips. A carom off an upright still counts if it continues through.
  • A miss or a ball that hits the table first is no good. After any score or missed kick, the other player starts from their edge.

Winning

  • First to 21, or most points when the five-minute clock expires.
  • A timed tie is broken by alternating field goals until someone leads after an equal number of kicks.
